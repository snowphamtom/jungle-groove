# Jungle Groove
## Toca Boca × Lego Marvel × Madagascar/Zootopia — Design Variant

**Working title:** Jungle Groove  
**Platform focus:** iPad (touch-first, landscape + portrait)  
**Tone:** Warm, silly, stylish, zero violence — pure play

---

### One-line pitch
Dress up jungle animals like Toca Boca, free-roam a colorful jungle city like Lego Marvel, then drop them into a Madagascar/Zootopia-style dance party where outfits and music change the whole vibe — and everyone keeps dancing until you touch them.

---

### Core pillars

1. **Dress-up (Toca Boca DNA)**  
   - Instant, no-fail outfit changes  
   - Big friendly icons, one-tap apply  
   - Outfits are pure expression (tuxedo lion, disco zebra, chef parrot…)  
   - No stats, no “best” loadout

2. **Free-roam play (Lego Marvel DNA)**  
   - Open jungle-city hub you can wander  
   - Characters idle-dance in the world  
   - Interact by tapping / holding (pauses their dance)  
   - Side activities later: photo booth, snack cart, stage minigames  
   - Same energy as “run around as anyone and mess about”

3. **Dance party centerpiece (Madagascar + Zootopia)**  
   - Central dance floor with disco ball + jungle canopy  
   - Characters always move to the beat unless player is holding them  
   - Playlist changes tempo → dance speed changes  
   - Fashion + music combination = the “show”

### Systems

**Outfit system**  
Natural · Tuxedo · Hula · Shades · Hero Cape · Disco · Raincoat · Crown · Chef · Astro  

**Music system**  
Procedural tracks with BPM that drives animation speed:  
- Groove Safari (112)  
- Bamboo Bounce (128)  
- Moonlit Mambo (96)  
- Stampede Drop (140)  
- River Rhythm (104)

**Interaction rule**  
Default = dancing to music.  
On hold / drag = pause dance + face player (Toca-style “I’m listening”).  
Release = back to the beat.

---

### iPad UX

- Large touch targets (44px+)  
- Bottom dock: Dress Up · Music · Party · Mute  
- Bottom sheets for outfits & playlist  
- No text-heavy tutorials — hint chip then fade  
- Safe-area aware for notched devices  
- Works offline as a single HTML file

---

### Future expansion (Lego Marvel layer)

- Multi-zone hub: Canopy Club, Riverwalk, Tree Market, Cliff Stage  
- “Missions” that are pure play prompts (help Pip find a hat, start a conga line)  
- Photo mode with stickers  
- Co-op on same iPad (two fingers, two animals)  
- Seasonal outfits / music packs
